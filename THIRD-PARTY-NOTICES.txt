=============================

Dicteer is built from open-source software and ships some of it inside this
installation. This file says what is included, under which licence, and where
its source is. The complete text of the long licences sits in the licenses
folder next to this file.

5. Speech models
----------------

The Whisper models are not part of this installer. Dicteer downloads them on
first use, from the Hugging Face hub, into %APPDATA%\Dicteer\Models:
  faster-whisper (Systran) - MIT
  OpenAI Whisper weights - MIT
The Groq online backend, when you turn it on, processes audio on Groq's
servers under Groq's own terms and sends nothing else.
